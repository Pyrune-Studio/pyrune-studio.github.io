This policy explains how the Nth mobile app (the "App") handles personal data, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Acts 1988–2018.

**In short:** Nth has no account. Your counts stay on your phone and we never receive them. The only data that leaves your device is what is needed to sell and restore purchases: a random anonymous identifier and your purchase history.

## 1. Data controller

{company.legalName}, a company registered in Ireland. Contact: <a href={`mailto:${company.email}`}>{company.email}</a>. Full company details are on the [legal notice](/legal).

## 2. What stays on your phone

Everything you create in the App: what you count, the titles and names you use, your taps and history, your settings and the skins you choose. This data is stored only in the App's local database on your device. We never receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

- **Reminders** (for example "Remind me" for a new skin) are scheduled locally by your phone's operating system. Nothing is sent to us.
- **Share images** are only sent when you choose to share them, through your phone's share sheet, to the app you pick. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
- **Rating prompts** are shown by Apple or Google through their own system; we do not receive your rating or review from the App.

## 3. What leaves your phone: purchases

Nth sells optional items: individual skins, the Nth Club subscription and the Founders offer. To make these work, the following data is processed:

- **Payment details** are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card or bank details.
- **A random anonymous identifier**, created on your phone, is sent to RevenueCat and to our Founders server, to tell purchases apart and restore them.
- **Your purchase history** (product, date, store transaction ID, price, currency, renewals, cancellations and refunds) is sent to RevenueCat, to unlock what you bought, restore it and manage renewals and refunds.
- **Basic technical information** (app version, operating system version, device model, store country and currency) is sent to RevenueCat, to run the purchase service.
- **Your Founders seat**, if you buy Founders (your Founder number, purchase date, store transaction ID, refund date if any, and the anonymous identifier), is recorded on our Founders server, to show the number of seats left and your Founder number.
- **Your IP address** reaches RevenueCat and Cloudflare, as for any internet request, only to deliver the request. Our server does not store it.

The anonymous identifier is not linked by us to your name, email address or store account. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier, and the App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

When you open the App, it may also ask our Founders server for the number of Founders seats left. This request carries your anonymous identifier, so that the server can return your Founder number if you have one.

## 4. Purposes and legal bases

- **Selling, unlocking and restoring purchases, managing subscriptions and refunds**: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Numbering Founders and showing the seat counter**: performance of a contract, as the Founders offer is limited in number (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Preventing fraud and securing our server**: our legitimate interest in protecting our service and our users (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Answering your messages**: our legitimate interest in helping our users (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Keeping transaction records**: compliance with our accounting and tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

## 5. Processors

We use the following services, which process data on our behalf and only to provide their service to us:

- **RevenueCat, Inc.** (purchase management): [privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).
- **Cloudflare, Inc.** (hosting of the Founders server and its database): [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Payments are handled by **Google** (Google Play) and **Apple** (App Store) as independent controllers, under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

## 6. Retention

- **Data on your device**: until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.
- **Purchase history and anonymous identifier at RevenueCat**: as long as the App's purchase service is operated, so that your purchases can be restored, and then as required by our legal obligations.
- **Founders seat**: as long as the Founders offer exists, since it grants lifetime access.
- **Emails you send us**: deleted no later than 24 months after the last exchange.

## 7. Transfers outside the EU

RevenueCat and Cloudflare are based in the United States. Transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, write to <a href={`mailto:${company.email}`}>{company.email}</a>. We answer within one month.

As the App has no account, we cannot identify you on our own. To help us find your records, include your store order number (shown in your Google Play or App Store receipt) and, for Founders, your Founder number. On request, we can detach your anonymous identifier and your Founders seat from our records. Your purchases remain valid with your store account. Transaction records we must keep by law are retained for the legal period.

The data on your phone is under your control: you can delete any count in the App, or uninstall the App to delete everything.

## 9. Complaints

You can lodge a complaint with the Irish [Data Protection Commission](https://www.dataprotection.ie) or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

## 10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16. It does not ask for any personal information, and purchases are made through the parent-controlled payment systems of Apple and Google.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy, for example if the App gains new features. The date at the top of the page shows the latest revision; significant changes will also be announced in the App. See also the [studio privacy policy](/privacy) and the [Nth Terms of Use](/apps/nth/terms).
